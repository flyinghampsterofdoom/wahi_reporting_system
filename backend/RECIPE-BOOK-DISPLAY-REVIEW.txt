Findings
151 Swizzle has four preparation steps in BOTH its saved bartender draft and its immutable published version, plus garnish “Cinnamon Stick & Sprinkle of Nutmeg” and glassware “Swizzle Cup”. The missing instructions are a presentation problem, not missing saved recipe data.
Two separate omissions caused it: serviceDocument() replaced all instruction arrays with empty arrays in the published response; recipe-renderer.js ignored instructions entirely. The current production response has zero instruction rows although its saved publication contains six (four preparation, one garnish, one glassware).
Simple Syrup is a 0.5 oz ingredient in the drink's saved ingredient list. A separate child recipe component stores its copied underlying formula. The renderer recursively displayed that child as a details/summary element, producing the unwanted collapsible Simple Syrup section.
