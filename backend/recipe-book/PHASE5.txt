1. Component collapsing
Every ingredient, preparation, garnish, glassware, notes and recipe editing card now has an
accessible title toggle with aria-expanded/aria-controls. Nested cards use the same control.
Collapse/Expand all controls are provided. Drag handles, Move up/down and removal controls stay
outside the folded fields. Keyboard focus follows reordered blocks, and folding survives reorder.
Titles update on the toggle as they are edited. Archived entries can still be folded for review.

Editing folds and nested editing-group folds are transient UI state keyed by component ID. They
do not alter service documents, dirty state, version, current publication, source or costing.
Bartender expansion is an explicit, separately labeled checkbox on recipe components. This
replaces the former implicit coupling between the nested editor accordion and service expansion.
A collapsed editor therefore cannot accidentally hide service content in a later publication.

2. Complete bartender preview

Recipe Book markup, controls, alphabetical search/menu filtering, selection/rendering and image
path. recipeBookMarkup/recipeBookControls are shared with the Book; recipePaintList/recipeSelect
and WahiRecipeRenderer.render are reused. There is no second service renderer or copied preview
layout. It uses the existing 20/45/35 columns, protected-image behavior, aspect ratio and placeholder.

The selected management draft replaces its own catalog row in this private preview; other rows

catalog rows while keeping the explicit draft preview. Opening/closing does not save or publish,
change menu classification, or write draft/images to offline storage. Preview state has no offline
lease, and photo object URLs are revoked on close. Escape/Close restore focus to the Builder action.
Station identities have no Builder/management-preview route or access to its draft/media APIs.
