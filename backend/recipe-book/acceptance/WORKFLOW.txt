Evidence lineage
source-data.json is a narrow projection from the preserved local import capture. Its selected
IDs/revisions and every ingredient line (including dependency recipes) were checked against the
approved, previously captured source inventory. The original workbook hash, capture hashes and
inventory time are recorded in source-data.json. It contains 5 selections, 12 recipes including
house mixes, and 51 ingredient identities. No source prices, inventory, labor, Toast data, users,
service instructions or photographs were copied into that projection. This is a point-in-time
acceptance fixture, not a claim that production has been checked again in Phase 5.

prepare.cjs can reproduce the projection from the two existing captures without database access:
  node backend/recipe-book/acceptance/prepare.cjs <captured-import-result.json> <captured-inventory.json> <output.json>
Do not replace the committed fixture automatically with new source data. Review identity/revision
and ingredient changes explicitly. The extraction rejects identity/ingredient mismatches and
unexpected instructions instead of choosing or inventing content.

Controlled local workflow
1. From the isolated worktree, install dependencies and ensure local PostgreSQL 16 tools exist.
   Start: node backend/recipe-book/acceptance/review.cjs
   It creates an ephemeral private-socket database, applies the committed migration manifest,
   loads only the verified source projection, and prepares five private Off Menu service drafts.
   It never uses WAHI_DATABASE_URL, hosted configuration, or a production service. Its fixture
   identities are disposable test records, not provisioning of existing or production accounts.
2. Open the printed local URL. Use fixture.manager for management entry or fixture.owner for
   Admin recovery checks. The terminal prints the synthetic fixture password. Keep the process
   open while reviewing. Only local fixtures use these identities/passwords.
3. Select each saved draft by cocktail title. Confirm source IDs/revisions and ingredient names,
   quantities, units, notes and nested mixes against the captured source. Quantities are retained
   exactly; notably Navy Grog/Zombie bitters retain 0.0062500000000000003 fl_oz_us and the original
   '1 Dash' note. Management must approve the visible service measurement; no automatic rounding
   or change to costing has occurred.
4. Obtain the actual house preparation from management. Add a Preparation block and instructions
   through the Builder. Add separately approved garnish and glassware details. Ingredient rows
   that happen to mention mint/lime do not automatically establish service garnish policy.
   The content-status message identifies missing fields. Empty instructions remain valid draft
   content but do not pass publication. Do not publish placeholder instructions.
5. Upload the real corresponding cocktail photograph if available. Preview before saving, verify
   association and orientation, and review it in Full-size bartender preview. If unavailable,
   leave photoId empty and record 'photo pending'; the placeholder/warning is expected and does
   not block publication. Never attach synthetic test images to these real cocktails.
6. Save Draft. Confirm both wells still see only the previous publication (or no entry). Open
   Full-size bartender preview: it uses the actual service layout/renderer and displays this
   unsaved/saved draft privately. It neither publishes nor stores private draft data offline.
7. After management validates the authored content, explicitly Publish. Confirm On Menu/Off Menu
   stays unchanged. Classify separately as management decides. Record publication ID/version,
   reviewer, review time, ingredient discrepancies, real photo availability and source revision.
8. Log in independently as well.one and well.two in separate browser/device profiles. Verify all
   five completed recipes' names, ingredients, instructions, garnish, glassware and corresponding
   photographs/placeholders. Confirm direct Recipe Book landing, recipe-only navigation, search,
   menu filters and denial of Builder/admin/draft APIs. Record desktop and tablet results.
9. Edit a draft; verify wells retain the current immutable publication. Replace/remove a photo
   and verify the published photo stays intact until another publication. As Admin, restore an
   earlier publication into draft; confirm current publication/menu status do not change. Preview
   the recovered historical photo, then explicitly publish if desired. Test service archive and
   restore separately. Do not mutate costing recipes, inventory mappings or Toast records.
10. Exit with Ctrl-C. The review tool exports a private database.dump, media directory (including
    originals/derivatives/manifests), and human-readable drafts.json under the printed unique
    .recipe-private/phase5/owner-review checkpoint before removing the disposable cluster. If
    checkpointing fails, it retains the running environment for recovery and reports the error.
    The dump is the restore authority; drafts.json is a later convenience export. Do not close
    the terminal forcibly before checkpoint completion. These checkpoints are local/private and
    must not be committed or copied into production as account/migration provisioning shortcuts.
