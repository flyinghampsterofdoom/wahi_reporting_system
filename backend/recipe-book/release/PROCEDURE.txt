Media policy and costs
New adapter uses the EXISTING mounted volume; incremental fixed infrastructure/subscription cost $0.
Normal existing compute/database/network rates still apply. No resource was purchased or resized.
A 512 MiB media-tree cap and 128 MiB free-disk reserve bound growth; upload exhaustion returns a clear
message, never deletes history, and never automatically expands paid capacity. The observed disk had
978188 KiB available and ~2208 KiB used. Any later expansion/provider change needs separate cost approval.
Directories are 0700; original/display/integrity files 0600. JPEG/PNG/WebP are actually decoded and
validated, <=8 MiB and <=24M pixels/single frame; display images fit inside 1400x1600 without cropping.
Originals and published associations are immutable. Private delivery is through existing authenticated
routes only; no static file/public URL exposes originals. Mount absence fails closed for hosted storage.
